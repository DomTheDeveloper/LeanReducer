## The arithmetic statement to replace

Lean proves `FixedClassRH.ofFiniteGain : FixedClassRH.FiniteGainEstimate → RiemannHypothesis`. The existing covolume argument supplies an equivalent estimate and gives a reference proof against which to test a shorter one. All newly imported project source counts toward the replacement budget. [Finite-gain criterion](strategy/RHFiniteGain.lean) · [Covolume proof](strategy/RHNormalizedSupply.lean)

Fix the modulus $M=18480$ and an auxiliary prime $d\equiv9239\pmod M$. Write $R_M$ and $R_1$ for the two normalized prime errors at this prime. Auxiliary data provide scales $x\le y$, $L=\log b$, an integer $m\ge\sqrt{x}$, and a weight $w$ equal to the tower degree divided by the number of thick places. The construction chooses the weight before selecting the second scale. The comparison is needed on the indicated logarithmic windows where $R_M(y)-wR_1(y)\le e^{-10^6\log x}$. The cutoff may depend on $d$.

Take $A=\lceil y\rceil$ and $v^+=\max(v,0)$. The sum $G(x,y)$ has nonnegative terms

$$
\frac{\lambda_{d,M}(n)}{A}\left[\left(\frac A n-\frac A y\right)^+-\left(\frac A n-\frac A x\right)^+\right],\qquad 1\le n\le A .
$$

The identity $R_M(x)-R_M(y)=(\log y-\log x)/3840-G(x,y)$ cancels the modulus-$M$ logarithmic-derivative constant. The outstanding bound is

$$
G(x,y)\ \ge\ T=\frac{\log y-\log x}{3840}+wR_1(y)-\frac{3840\,wL^5}{m}
$$

whenever $T>0$ on the favorable windows. Nonnegativity settles $T\le0$. The Lean bound includes the modulus-one remainder and applies on favorable windows.

## Implementation sequence

1. Use `lower_bound_from_subset` to insert pointwise lower bounds on a chosen finite subset of $1,\ldots,A$. The checked block formula splits the weights at $x$. For $1\le n\le x$ the term equals $\lambda_{d,M}(n)(1/x-1/y)$. For $x<n\le y$ it equals $\lambda_{d,M}(n)(1/n-1/y)$, and for $n>y$ it vanishes. Start with primes $p\equiv1\pmod {18480}$ with Legendre symbol $(p/d)=-1$. Each contributes $2\log p$ times its displayed coefficient. The theorem `inertPrimeBlocks_le` packages the contribution of any selected finite set of these primes. Contributions of the other terms are nonnegative. Retain the modulus-one correction when combining estimates. A fixed relative loss on the main term grows like $\log(y/x)$, while the allowed error $3840\,wL^5/m$ can be bounded by $3840x^{6\varepsilon-1/2}$ on the data used for RH. The proof must work for arbitrarily large $x$ at each required prime. [Block lemmas](strategy/RHGainBlocks.lean)
2. If a direct arithmetic lower bound stalls, test a determinant on the same weighted gain. The available theorem bounds the height of a ratio of Gram determinants after a family is completed to a basis. For the same family, prove a lower bound on the logarithmic height and an upper bound in terms of the two-block prime sum that together imply $G\ge T$. The index and basis-completion costs must fit the displayed error. If the missing step is the rank of the translates, the filling-moments theorem supplies that kind of estimate at the higher source cost below.
3. Put the new proof of `FiniteGainEstimate` in a module importing `RHGainBlocks` and the arithmetic lemmas it needs. Keep the covolume proof in the separate comparison test. At each milestone, run `python3 -m leanreducer analyze path/to/src --entry MODULE` on the proposed endpoint. Count the union of all newly required modules. After the supplier compiles, apply `ofFiniteGain`, check the theorem against the exact Mathlib type, inspect the axiom report, replay changed modules and the entry with `leanchecker`, then rebuild the complete measured source from a clean checkout. ## How much can pruning save?

The kernel-expression walk from `FixedClassRH.ordinaryRH` reaches **37,550 project declarations in all 793 local modules**. Removing whole modules whose declarations are unused yields zero lines on this proof. Available source ranges for the reached declarations cover **64,358 distinct code lines**, even after omitting upstream ranges in 21 changed modules. Lean reported no source range for 24,857 reached declarations. Keeping all mapped declaration bodies intact and deleting every line outside them would still exceed 49,999 by **14,359** lines.
